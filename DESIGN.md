# Design System

## Visual Direction

Cinematic lunar editorial.

The design should feel:
- premium
- quiet
- technical
- mysterious
- spatial
- sophisticated

It should NOT feel:
- cyberpunk
- gaming
- SaaS
- cryptocurrency
- generic AI
