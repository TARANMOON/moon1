# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — Taran Moon

## 1. Executive Summary
**Taran Moon** is a cinematic, editorial, and technical personal portfolio for a Creative Technologist and Systems Architect. It departs from standard generic SaaS/AI templates to deliver a continuous, scroll-driven lunar odyssey that showcases world-class craft in frontend architecture, canvas rendering, spatial interactivity, and editorial design.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
- **Narrative First**: The visitor experiences an immersive journey from deep space orbit down to the cratered lunar surface before transitioning into high-density technical achievements and selected works.
- **Craft as Proof**: The medium is the message—the performance, responsiveness, and smoothness of the 240-frame canvas engine directly demonstrates technical mastery.

## 3. Target Audience & Visitor Persona
- **Design Engineers & Tech Leads**: Looking for uncompromising craft, architectural rigor, and deep attention to detail.
- **Founders & Visionary Executives**: Looking for creative technologists capable of bridging high-end art direction with performant code.
- **Creative Studios**: Looking for spatial, WebGL/Canvas, and motion expertise.

## 4. Key Experience Goals
1. **Zero-LCP Instant Perception**: The poster frame paints immediately, while the progressive asset loader caches frames smoothly in the background.
2. **Deterministic Scrubbing**: Every pixel scrolled corresponds linearly to lunar trajectory frames (0.00 -> Frame 1, 1.00 -> Frame 240).
3. **Editorial Typography**: Monochromatic, high-contrast display typography paired with technical monospaced telemetry data.
4. **Resilience & Accessibility**: Flawless degradation on low-power mobile devices and full support for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 5. Site Sections Breakdown
1. **Scene 01 — Orbit (0% – 15%)**: Cosmic arrival, telemetry initialization, minimalist hero branding ("TARAN MOON").
2. **Scene 02 — Approach (15% – 35%)**: Aggressive lunar scaling, creative technologist statement & positioning.
3. **Scene 03 — Descent (35% – 55%)**: Surface approach, technical disciplines & telemetry metrics.
4. **Scene 04 — Surface / Selected Works (55% – 75%)**: High-impact editorial project showcase with architecture highlights and live demos.
5. **Scene 05 — Capabilities & System Architecture (75% – 88%)**: Core technical competencies (Creative Technology, Canvas/Spatial, AI Systems, Full-Stack Architecture).
6. **Scene 06 — Contact / Transmission (88% – 100%)**: Direct transmission terminal, copy-to-clipboard email, encrypted status, and social channels.

## 6. Success Metrics
- 60fps continuous scroll-driven canvas rendering.
- Lighthouse Performance score 90+ on desktop, 80+ on mobile.
- Zero hydration errors, zero layout shifts (CLS < 0.05).
