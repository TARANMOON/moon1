# Motion Rules

- Master Timeline: Scroll position (0.0 to 1.0) directly scrub-controls the 240 lunar frames.
- Motion Stack: GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis smooth scrolling.
- Principles: Continuous, deliberate, restrained, zero arbitrary spring physics or random floating decorations.
- Reduced Motion: Provide graceful fallback for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
